# Code of Conduct

[中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [English](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md)

## Our commitment

So that everyone can take part without worry, we want this project to be welcoming to all, regardless of experience level, language, time zone, gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, race or religion.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique code, not people.
- Respect different languages and time zones; contributors who are not native Chinese speakers are making an effort too.
- Give reasons when you disagree on technical matters, and allow "I don't know yet".
- Do not publish other people's private information, including original photos, capture coordinates, device serial numbers or private chats.
- Do not manipulate stars, and do not use issues for promotion unrelated to the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexual innuendo, or unwelcome intimate attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Malicious submissions, destructive PRs, spam issues, or deliberately bypassing CI.
- Continuing any of the above after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

- Maintainers may edit, close or delete violating content, and may temporarily or permanently ban accounts when necessary.
- To report: use GitHub's "Report abuse", or contact the maintainer [@Eddie-Duan](https://github.com/Eddie-Duan) privately.
- Reports will be handled promptly and kept confidential as far as possible.
- The same rules apply to maintainers.

## Scope

This Code applies to issues, PRs, Discussions, code comments in this repository, and to public situations where you represent the project.

## Note

This Code draws on the spirit of Contributor Covenant 2.1 and is rewritten for this project; it is not a verbatim copy.
